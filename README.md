# BIMBY_2026_Field_Season

Code for cleaning and analysis of data collected in the field from May to September of 2026 at 25 sites ranging from Vancouver Island to the Okanagan.

**Raw Data** - File that contains the raw field data in the form of .CSV for the butterfly, flower, and nectaring data sets 
  1. **butterflies_raw**: .CSV includes the date, environmental conditions and results of 500 m butterfly surveys that took roughly 15 minutes.  Each row represents a species of butterfly found along a transect on a certain date. Columns includes month (numeric), day, week (ranging from 1-8), observer (who conducted the survey), transect (three letter code representing the transect name and location), the lat. and lon. (of the start of the transect in degrees), elevation (meters), time_beg (time the survey started), time_end (time the survey ended), temp_beg (temperature at the beginning of the survey in Celsius), temp_end (temperature at the end of the survey), air_quality (the AQI at the beginning of the survey), weather (sunny, partly cloudy, or cloudy), wind_beg (wind according to the Beaufort Scale at the beginning of the survey), wind_end (wind according to the Beaufort Scale at the end of the survey) family (taxonomic butterfly family), genus (taxonomic butterfly genus), species (taxonomic butterfly species), and number (the number of that species found during the survey).
  2. **flowers_raw**: .CSV includes the date, transect, quadrat and its location, then the flower type and species that was found within the 1 x 1 m quadrat. Each row represents a flowering plant species within 1-5 quadrats that were randomly selected each 100 m of the 500 m transect. Columns are as follows: month (numeric), day, week (ranging from 1 - 8), observer (who conducted the survey), transect (three letter code representing the transect name and location), quadrat (the quadrat sampled ranging from 1-5), location_m (the location in meters of where the quadrat was randomly selected), side (left or right side of the transect represented with "L" and "R" and randomly selected), num_units (number of floral units counted by observer), floral_unit (type of floral unit that was counted, including "infl" for inflorescence, head for grouped flowers like Asteraceae, flr for an individual flower, stm for stems of plants with small flowers like tansy and yarrow), unit_size (small, medium, or large based on flower sized and used for binning of filtered and cleaned data), cover_% (estimated percent cover of the 1 x 1 m quadrat for that floral species), colour (the colour that the majority of the "flower" is), family (the flowers taxonomic family), genus (the flowers taxonomic genus), species (the flowers taxonomic species), infraspecies (if a subspecies was applicable), origin (native or non-native), nectar (whether the flower produces nectar), area_cm_2 (rough floral area as calculated using quadrat photos on imagej), notes (any notes from the survey or changes made to the spreadsheet), and lookup_helper (used to speed up plant ID during data entry).
  3. **nectar_raw**: .CSV file with the nectaring observations of butterflies on flowers along transects. Nectaring observations were collected over the span of rougly 15 minutes. Nectaring observations were based on photos and field observations.
